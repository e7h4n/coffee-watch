Bring your
watch online.

Your brewing companion, now on your network. Set up Wi-Fi and Dropbox directly from Chrome over Bluetooth.

  1. 01

    Open setup on your watch

    On the idle screen, press Wi-Fi setup. Keep that screen open.

  2. 02

    Connect over Bluetooth

    Choose your Coffee Watch. If your computer asks for a pairing code, enter the six digits shown on the watch.

  3. 03

    Send your network details

    Use a 2.4 GHz network. The watch confirms when it has an IP address, then remembers the network.

  4. 04

    Link your Dropbox

    Create your own Dropbox app with App folder access, enter its key and secret, allow access on Dropbox and paste the code it shows. The watch verifies an upload in its app folder.

DROPBOX

In the Dropbox App Console ↗, create an app with Scoped access and App folder access. Under Permissions, enable files.content.write, files.content.read and files.metadata.read, then press Submit. Copy the App key and App secret from Settings.

Get authorization code on Dropbox ↗

Allow access on Dropbox, then copy the code it shows. Each code works once and expires after a few minutes.

Credentials travel over authenticated Bluetooth. The watch exchanges the code with Dropbox directly and keeps a refresh token, so uploads keep working. This page does not upload or store them.

Dropbox not connected

Connect Wi-Fi first, then authorize your watch.

Check uploads connection-check.json to the app folder (Dropbox › Apps › your app name) and compares Dropbox’s content hash with the watch’s. Removing erases credentials from the watch; to revoke access, disconnect the app under Connected apps in your Dropbox settings.

Your credentials go directly to the watch over authenticated Bluetooth.
They are never uploaded to GitHub or saved by this page.
