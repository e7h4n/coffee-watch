Your brewing companion, now on your network. Set up Wi-Fi directly from Chrome over Bluetooth.

  1. 01

    Open setup on your watch

    On the idle screen, press Wi-Fi setup. Keep that screen open.

  2. 02

    Connect over Bluetooth

    Choose your Coffee Watch. If your computer asks for a pairing code, enter the six digits shown on the watch.

  3. 03

    Send your network details

    Use a 2.4 GHz network. The watch confirms when it has an IP address, then remembers the network.
